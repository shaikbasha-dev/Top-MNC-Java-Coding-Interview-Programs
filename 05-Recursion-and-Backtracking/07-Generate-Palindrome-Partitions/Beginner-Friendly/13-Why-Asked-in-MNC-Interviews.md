# 13 — Why Asked in MNC Interviews

## Why Interviewers Ask This Problem

**Generate palindrome partitions** tests whether a candidate can recognize a decision-tree problem and implement recursion or backtracking without losing or corrupting state.

## Skills Evaluated

1. Recognition of recursive structure.
2. Correct base-case design.
3. Choice exploration.
4. Backtracking and state restoration.
5. Boundary-condition handling.
6. Complexity analysis.
7. Ability to optimize unnecessary repeated work.
8. Ability to explain a recursion tree.

## Common Follow-Ups

- What is the base case?
- What is the recurrence?
- Why must the state be restored?
- What happens if the base case is missing?
- What is the recursion depth?
- Can memoization or pruning improve the solution?
