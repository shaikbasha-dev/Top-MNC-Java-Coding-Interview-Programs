# 13 — Why Asked in MNC Interviews

## Why Interviewers Ask This Problem

**Inorder, preorder and postorder traversal** tests fundamental data-structure knowledge and the ability to reason about hierarchical or connected data.

## Skills Evaluated

1. Tree/graph representation.
2. Recursion.
3. Traversal order.
4. Queue usage.
5. DFS/BFS understanding.
6. BST property.
7. Visited-state management.
8. Complexity analysis.
9. Edge-case handling.
10. Ability to explain the algorithm clearly.

## Common Follow-Ups

- What happens for an empty tree?
- Can this be solved iteratively?
- What is the time complexity?
- What is the maximum recursion depth?
- Why is a queue used for BFS?
- Why is parent tracking needed for an undirected cycle check?
