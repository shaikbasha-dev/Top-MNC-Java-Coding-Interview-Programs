# Why This Problem Is Asked in MNC Interviews — Maximum Sum Contiguous Subarray

## Why Interviewers Ask This Problem

This problem tests whether a candidate can convert a natural-language requirement into a working algorithm.

## Skills Being Tested

1. Understanding the problem statement.
2. Working with arrays or matrices.
3. Using loops correctly.
4. Using conditions correctly.
5. Tracking important values.
6. Avoiding unnecessary operations.
7. Explaining time and space complexity.
8. Writing readable Java code.

## What the Interviewer Wants to See

The interviewer is not only interested in the final answer. They want to see whether you can explain:

- What you are going to do.
- Why your approach works.
- What variables you need.
- What happens in each iteration.
- What the complexity is.
- How the solution can be optimized.

## Interview Tip

First explain the simple idea clearly. Then discuss the optimized approach if the interviewer asks for better time or space complexity.
