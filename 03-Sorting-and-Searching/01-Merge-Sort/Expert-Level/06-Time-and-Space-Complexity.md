# 06 — Time and Space Complexity

## Beginner-Friendly Approach

**Time Complexity:** `O(n log n)`

**Space Complexity:** `O(n) auxiliary merge space plus recursion stack.`

## Why

The time complexity is dominated by the primary operation such as sorting, binary searching, scanning, heap maintenance or recursive partitioning.

## Expert-Level Perspective

The expert implementation is designed around the strongest structural property available in the problem. The objective is not merely shorter code; it is reducing unnecessary work.

## Interview Answer

When asked for complexity, state:

1. Time complexity.
2. Auxiliary space complexity.
3. What operation dominates the complexity.
4. Whether the bound is average-case or worst-case when that distinction matters.
