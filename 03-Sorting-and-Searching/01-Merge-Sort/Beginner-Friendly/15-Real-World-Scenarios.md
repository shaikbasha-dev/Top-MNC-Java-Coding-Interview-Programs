# 15 — Real-World Scenarios

## Scenario 1 — Data Processing

Algorithms like this are useful when large collections of records must be organized, searched or grouped efficiently.

## Scenario 2 — Search Systems

Efficient searching reduces the amount of data that must be inspected before finding a requested value.

## Scenario 3 — Analytics

Sorting, ranking, interval merging and median calculations appear in reporting, statistics and data-analysis systems.

## Scenario 4 — Scheduling and Range Processing

Interval-based algorithms are useful for calendars, reservations, time windows and resource allocation.

## Scenario 5 — Interview-to-Production Thinking

The interview problem is simplified, but the underlying idea teaches an important production skill: choose an algorithm whose complexity matches the size and structure of the data.
